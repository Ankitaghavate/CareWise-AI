## 📖 Introduction
This system simplifies the college hunt by analyzing historical cut-off data. By using a **Random Forest Classifier**, it predicts the probability of admission into specific engineering programs based on the user's percentage, preferred course, and admission category (CAP/Merit).

---

## 🚀 Features
* ✨ **Smart Recommendations:** Suggests the **Top 10 colleges** based on your score.
* 📉 **Risk Assessment:** Highlights colleges where your admission probability is low.
* 🧠 **Natural Language Processing:** Parses user queries like `"Suggest colleges for 88% Electrical Engineering CAP"`.
* 📊 **Probability Scoring:** Provides a confidence score for every recommendation.
